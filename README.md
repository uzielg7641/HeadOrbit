## 🧭 What is HeadOrbit?

HeadOrbit is a small helper app that lives in your menu bar. It watches how you hold your head and reminds you to sit up straight. It also blurs your screen when you look away – perfect for privacy or quick focus breaks.

Your AirPods already know where you're looking. HeadOrbit simply puts that information to good use.

## 📋 What Does HeadOrbit Do?

HeadOrbit has three main jobs:

### 1. Look‑Away Blur

When you glance away from your screen, HeadOrbit automatically blurs your display. This keeps sensitive information hidden and helps you stay focused.

- Blur activates when you look away for a set time (usually 1–2 seconds).
- The blur clears instantly when you look back.

### 2. Posture Reminder

Sitting hunched over? HeadOrbit detects when your head tilts too far forward or to the side. You’ll get a gentle notification to sit up straight.

- You choose how strict the reminder is.
- Adjust the sensitivity in settings to match your natural posture.

### 3. Menu Bar Status

The menu bar icon shows a green dot when head tracking is active. If it turns grey, check that your AirPods are still connected.

## ⚙️ Setting Your Preferences

HeadOrbit is easy to customize. Click the menu bar icon and select **Preferences** to adjust:

- **Blur Delay** – How long you must look away before the blur kicks in (0.5 to 5 seconds).
- **Blur Strength** – From a light frost to full opacity.
- **Posture Threshold** – How much forward tilt triggers a reminder (gentle, moderate, strict).
- **Notification Sound** – Choose a subtle chime or go silent.
- **Start at Login** – Launch HeadOrbit automatically when you turn on your Mac.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

### HeadOrbit Doesn’t Detect My AirPods

1. Open your Bluetooth settings.
2. Make sure your AirPods are connected and not in their case.
3. Under your AirPods name, verify that **Head Tracking** is enabled.
4. Restart HeadOrbit from the menu bar.

### The Blur Happens Too Often

Lower the sensitivity in Preferences. Set the blur delay to a longer time so it only activates when you look away for a while.

### Posture Reminders Are Too Frequent

Choose the "Gentle" threshold. This only warns you when your head is clearly leaning far forward.

### The App Won’t Open

- Go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**.
- Scroll down to the security section.
- Click **Open Anyway** next to HeadOrbit.

### HeadOrbit Isn’t in My Menu Bar

Check for the icon in the top‑right corner. If it’s missing, open the Applications folder and double‑click HeadOrbit again.

No. HeadOrbit is built specifically for macOS. It uses Apple’s native sensors inside AirPods to work reliably.

Yes. Open Preferences and set the blur strength to zero. Or disable the blur feature entirely.

### Will HeadOrbit drain my AirPods battery?

### Is my head movement data sent anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your device. HeadOrbit does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
